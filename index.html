Staying at Soysambu

Waiver & tents

Please complete this online waiver, which is mandatory for everyone visiting Soysambu. One person can submit for your group, but every (adult) guest needs to add their own signature. If you have rented a safari tent, you can also pay through the details in this form. Payment is needed to confirm your tent.

Please double check your dates before submitting. We'll use these to finalise the camp arrangements, tent allocations and payments, so please make sure they reflect your plans. If you have any questions before finalising, please feel free to ask Max or Sanne directly.

For international guests: Once you have sent the form, you can download a booking confirmation for your Kenya eTA, whether or not you have a tent. It is one standard confirmation with the same dates for everyone. You should only have to upload one booking confirmation document.
